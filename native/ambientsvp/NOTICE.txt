The original smooth.wgsl is included unmodified. The Android C ABI, bounded
streaming adapter and native wgpu/Vulkan host implementation are app additions.
The MPV and VLC filter adapters are LGPL-2.1-or-later.

This is an independent integration of Open-SVPFlow, not the proprietary SVP
Manager, SVP Android application, or a distribution of proprietary SVP binaries.
No VapourSynth, Python, OpenCL runtime or AVX-capable CPU is required.

All resolved Rust dependency names, versions, declared SPDX licenses and
repository URLs are in dependencies.tsv; their supplied license and notice
files are included beside it in the packaged licenses/open-svpflow asset.
