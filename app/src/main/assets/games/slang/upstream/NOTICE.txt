Authentic Libretro Slang upscalers bundled with Ambient TV
========================================================

Source repository: https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders
Pinned commit: 02bf134555c4595bd6b69be89feaff923da83754

All .slang and .slangp files below this directory are unmodified files from
that commit, preserving their upstream directory structure. PROVENANCE.json
records the exact SHA-256 and byte length of every vendored source/preset.
Only the listed presets and their complete dependencies are included; this
is not the complete RetroArch shader collection. No assets are downloaded
when a game starts.

The app's CC0 license does not replace these third-party license terms.
Original notices remain in the corresponding shader sources. GPL-2.0.txt
and GPL-3.0.txt include the complete GNU General Public License texts.

The same license texts also accompany Ambient TV's GLES adaptations of
these algorithms. Their original GLSL sources are pinned to:
  https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/a35069864b4bf30f8c8e740d57206543fb7e745e
Those adaptations change the renderer interface while retaining the
original algorithm and notices. The reproducible source manifest and
adaptation details are in third_party_assets/game_shaders in Ambient TV's
source repository. Super2xSaI's GLES adaptation uses the Slang source at
the Slang commit stated above. GLES and Slang use their respective upstream
presentation passes; they are not promised to produce identical pixels.
