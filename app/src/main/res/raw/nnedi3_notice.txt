NNEDI3 shader resources used by this app are taken from bjin/mpv-prescalers at
commit b3f0a59d68f33b7162051ea5970a5169558f0ea2.

Quality ladder: nns16, nns32, nns64, nns128 and nns256 use win8x4; the explicit
Expert entry nns256/win8x6 is bundled as well. All six presets come from this
pinned revision. The player validates the NNEDI3 stages before loading the copied
resource into mpv; no shader network fetch is required at playback time.
