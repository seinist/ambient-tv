    Why?

    Ambient TV started with a few things I wanted for my own movie nights: wake the server from the couch, choose the player and adjust the picture to suit the film.

    It grew into one place for media libraries, network folders and live TV, with a remote-friendly interface on TV and touch controls on phones and tablets.

    What's inside

    • 📚

      All sources, one app

      Bring Plex, Jellyfin and Emby libraries together with YouTube through SmartTube, Spotify and DLNA sources.

    • 📱

      Phone & TV together

      Pair by QR code on the same home network. Use your phone as a touchpad or keyboard, start supported content on TV, and transfer accounts and settings with confirmation. Keep Ambient TV open on both devices.

    • 📂

      Files without a media server

      Open local folders or connect via SMB, NFS, WebDAV or SFTP. Browse your own videos and music directly on the NAS or network storage.

    • ▶️

      MPV · VLC · Media3

      Three engines in one shared player, picture settings remembered per title.

    • ✨

      Upscalers & SVP

      GPU shader upscalers like NNEDI3, RAVU, FSRCNNX, Anime4K, FSR – plus experimental SVP motion smoothing.

    • 🌈

      HDR

      Display-mode matching, HDR tone mapping and optional SDR → HDR10 (Android 14+).

    • ⏰

      Wake-on-LAN

      Wakes sleeping servers and NAS sources before connecting.

    • 🛡️

      WireGuard on the go

      Built-in WireGuard, switchable per system – your libraries away from home.

    • 📺

      Live TV, at your pace

      TVHeadend and IPTV playlists with a programme guide and reminders. Enable timeshift to pause and rewind supported MPEG-TS streams; schedule, edit and delete recordings and timers.

    • 🏠

      A home screen of your own

      Arrange Continue watching, recently added titles, favourite channels and library shortcuts in the order that works for you.

    • 💬

      Subtitles that fit

      Choose preferred languages, load subtitle files beside your videos and adjust their timing. Online subtitle search is available with your own OpenSubtitles API key.

    • 🎵

      Music & photos

      Browse music folders with album tags and cover images, try spectrum or projectM visualisations, or show photos as a slideshow with gentle pans and zooms.

    Spotify setup

    Ambient TV does not include a Spotify client ID. Create your own in the Spotify Developer Dashboard and enter it in the app to sign in.

    1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard, choose Create app, enter a name and description, and select Web API.
    2. Add and save both redirect URIs:
      • ambienttv://spotify-auth — sign-in on the device
      • https://seinist.github.io/ambient-tv-spotify-callback/spotify-callback.html — QR sign-in on TV; the page forwards the code to the TV on your local network
    3. Copy the Client ID from your Spotify app's Settings.
    4. In Ambient TV, open Settings › Media sources & accounts › Spotify › Spotify account, enter your Spotify client ID, then choose Sign in.
