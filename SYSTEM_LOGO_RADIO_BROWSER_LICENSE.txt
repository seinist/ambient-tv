RadioBrowser system logo
========================

The RadioBrowser system mark in this repository
(app/src/main/res/drawable/system_radio.xml and
app/src/main/res/drawable/logo_radio_browser.xml)
is a vectorized adaptation of the official RadioBrowser logo.

Upstream source:
https://gitlab.com/radiobrowser/radiobrowser-web-angular/-/blob/main/src/favicon.ico

Reference:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RadioBrowser_logo.png

Author: RadioBrowser

The upstream logo is published under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 or later. The vector adaptation is modified for Ambient TV
(system-sized monochrome and colored Android drawable variants) and is
distributed under the same license.

A full copy of GPLv3 is already included in this repository in
SYSTEM_LOGO_TVHEADEND_LICENSE.txt and third_party_licenses/image_shaders/GPL-3.0.txt.
