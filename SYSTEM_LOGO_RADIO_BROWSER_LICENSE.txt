RadioBrowser logo assets
========================

Ambient TV uses the RadioBrowser logo for the built-in Radio system.

Files:
- app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/logo_radio_browser_official.png
- app/src/main/res/drawable/system_radio.xml

The colored PNG is the RadioBrowser globe-with-headphones logo. The copy used here
comes from the GPLv3 Kodi RadioBrowser client repository:
https://github.com/segler-alex/kodi-radio-browser/blob/master/icon.png

RadioBrowser's logo is also documented by Wikimedia Commons as authored by
RadioBrowser, sourced from the RadioBrowser web frontend, and licensed under
GNU GPL version 3 or later:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RadioBrowser_logo.png
https://gitlab.com/radiobrowser/radiobrowser-web-angular/-/blob/main/src/favicon.ico

The monochrome Android vector is a simplified small-size adaptation of that same
globe-with-headphones mark for Ambient TV menus, Home rows, badges and headings.

These logo assets are distributed under GNU GPL version 3 or later. A full copy of
GPLv3 is already included in this repository in SYSTEM_LOGO_TVHEADEND_LICENSE.txt
and third_party_licenses/image_shaders/GPL-3.0.txt.
